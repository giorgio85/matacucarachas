## Introduction

Welcome to Mata Cucarachas ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile game application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **None.** Our game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other identifying data.

### Device Information
- **Device Sensors:** The game uses your device's accelerometer to detect motion for gameplay controls (shaking to stomp cockroaches).
- **Device Storage:** The game stores your best score locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
- **Audio Settings:** The game accesses your device's audio system to play sound effects.

### Gameplay Data
- **Local Scores:** Your current score and best score are stored only on your device.
- **Game Progress:** Level progression and game statistics are maintained locally.
- **Settings:** Any game preferences or settings are stored on your device only.

## How We Use Your Information

### Game Functionality
- **Motion Controls:** Accelerometer data is used solely to detect when you shake your device to trigger the stomping action in the game.
- **Score Tracking:** Local storage is used to remember your best score between game sessions.
- **Audio Experience:** Sound effects are played to enhance your gaming experience.
- **Haptic Feedback:** Device vibration provides tactile feedback when stomping cockroaches.

### No External Transmission
- **No Internet Connection:** Our game operates entirely offline and does not connect to external servers.
- **No Data Sharing:** We do not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or behavioral data.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- All game data is stored locally on your device using React Native's AsyncStorage.
- No data is transmitted over the internet or stored on external servers.
- Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

### Security Measures
- Since no data leaves your device, there are no external security risks.
- Local storage is protected by your device's built-in security features.
- We do not have access to any of your stored data.

## Third-Party Services

### Expo Framework
- Our game is built using the Expo framework, which provides development tools and libraries.
- Expo does not collect personal data from our app users.
- Expo's privacy policy can be found at: https://expo.dev/privacy

### Device Permissions
- **Accelerometer:** Required for motion controls (shaking to stomp)
- **Audio:** Required for sound effects
- **Storage:** Required to save game progress locally

## Children's Privacy

Our game is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Delete Data:** You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app.
- **Reset Progress:** You can reset your game progress by clearing the app's data in your device settings.
- **Opt Out:** Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to opt out of.

### Device Settings
- You can disable motion controls by turning off the accelerometer in your device settings.
- You can disable sound effects by muting your device or adjusting in-game audio settings.
- You can disable haptic feedback in your device settings.
